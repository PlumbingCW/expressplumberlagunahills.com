An older tract city with newer neighborhoods

The first Laguna Niguel tracts were completed in 1962, and the city incorporated on December 1, 1989. That mix of early 1960s homes and later neighborhoods such as Niguel Summit, Rancho Niguel and Bear Brand Ranch means plumbing ages vary a lot from one street to the next.

Homes from the earliest tracts may still have original drain lines and old supply pipe. Newer areas tend to have plastic drains and copper or PEX supply, and their problems are more often fixtures, valves and water heaters.

Before we arrive

A few minutes of preparation helps the technician start work the moment they walk in. These steps apply whether you called at noon or at 2 a.m.

Stop the water

Close the main valve or the shutoff for the fixture. Do not wait for the visit.

Clear the way

Move cars, boxes and pets so the crew can reach the water heater, the cleanout or the leak.

Stay out of the water

Keep clear of standing water near outlets, and switch off a dry breaker if it is safe.

Share the details

Tell the dispatcher your cross streets, the fixture, what you hear and what you have tried.
