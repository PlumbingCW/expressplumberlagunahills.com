For a business, see commercial plumbing. We give a clear price before work starts on every service.

Many problems fit more than one service. A slow main line may need cabling today and a camera inspection next week, and a hot water complaint may be a failed element or a tired tank. The technician explains the likely cause on the first visit, and you decide the next step with the numbers in front of you.

All of this work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). The city where the home sits decides which permits apply, and we check that before work begins.

Planned jobs can be scheduled around your day. Emergency jobs jump the line, and the dispatcher tells you the plan when you call, including what to do while you wait.
