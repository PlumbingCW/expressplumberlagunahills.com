When a faucet leak is an emergency

A faucet that will not shut off, a supply line that is spraying or a sink cabinet that is filling with water is an emergency, and our 24/7 line is open for that call. While you wait, close the shutoff valves under the sink and put a bowl under any drip.

Same-day and after-hours visits are available when a crew is free.

  1. Turn the small oval valves under the sink clockwise to close them, one for hot and one for cold.
  2. If the valves are stuck or leak, close the main water valve for the house instead.
  3. Empty the cabinet and dry the base so water does not soak into the wood.
  4. Take a photo of the faucet label or model number to share when you call.

What causes a faucet to leak

Most faucets leak because an internal part has worn. Cartridge faucets use a replaceable cartridge, and ball and ceramic disk styles each have their own seats and seals. Mineral scale can also stop a valve from sealing.

Water in this area is very hard by USGS standards, with El Toro Water District reporting 236 to 293 mg/L in its 2026 report. Scale on aerators and cartridges is a common reason for a faucet that drips or sprays unevenly.

Repair or replace the faucet

An older faucet with a worn cartridge is often worth repairing if parts are available. A faucet with corroded or pitted metal, or one whose maker no longer sells parts, is better replaced.

We show you both options when the choice is close, and we can supply a faucet or install one you have bought.

Under sink leaks

  • Supply lines that are braided and aging, or that were over tightened.
  • Shutoff valves that weep at the stem.
  • P traps that have loosened or cracked.
  • Disposal and dishwasher connections that drip.
  • Drain baskets with a failed seal.

Permits

Laguna Hills publishes a handout of common projects exempt from permits, and it lists faucet or toilet repair or replacement as exempt. Laguna Woods Village guidance says sinks and faucets need a city permit, so tell us if the home is there.

Work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). We give a clear price before starting, and the final price depends on parts and the hour of the visit.

Making the repair last

Replacing worn shutoff valves during a faucet repair is a small cost compared with a flood later. Old valves often fail the first time they are turned after years of sitting. A leak detection visit helps if the damp area has no clear source.

Faucet Repair questions, answered

Do you handle faucet emergencies at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. Close the valves under the sink, or the main valve if they are stuck, and call.

Can you repair a single handle kitchen faucet?

Yes. Most single handle faucets use a replaceable cartridge, and we carry common types.

Why does my faucet spray sideways?

Scale in the aerator is the usual cause in hard water. Cleaning or replacing the aerator often fixes it.

Do I need a permit to replace a faucet?

The Laguna Hills handout of common projects exempt from permits lists faucet repair or replacement. Laguna Woods Village follows stricter guidance, so ask first.
