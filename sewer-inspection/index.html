Sewer Camera Inspection in Laguna Hills, CA

A sewer camera shows what is inside the pipe, so you pay for the right repair and not a guess. Call to schedule an inspection, and we quote the price before the camera goes in.

A sewer camera inspection puts a waterproof camera on a flexible rod and pushes it through the main line from the cleanout. The picture appears on a monitor as the camera moves. The tech can see the pipe wall, the joints and any water sitting in low spots.

It is the cleanest way to answer questions that a cable cannot, such as why a line keeps clogging or whether an older pipe is still sound.

When an inspection earns its cost

  • Before buying a home, especially one built in the decades after the early 1960s land subdivisions here.
  • After repeated main line clogs that cabling only fixed for a short time.
  • Before landscaping, pool or hardscape work over the sewer path.
  • When a damp patch or sewage smell appears in the yard.
  • After a repair, to confirm the new section drains properly.

What the camera can and cannot tell you

The camera reveals root intrusion, cracked or offset joints, scale, grease, bellies and collapsed sections. It cannot see through a full blockage, so the line is usually cleared first.

A locator on the camera head lets the tech mark the depth and the surface position of each defect. That helps you see how a repair would affect the yard before you agree to it.

How an inspection runs

  1. Access

    We open the cleanout and check the line is clear enough for the camera.

  2. Record

    The camera runs the length of the line while the monitor shows the live picture.

  3. Locate

    A locator marks the depth and ground position of anything that needs attention.

  4. Review

    We go through the findings with you and explain which are minor and which need repair.

Inspections for home buyers in Laguna Hills

In most cases the sewer lateral is the property owner's responsibility from the house to the connection at the street or main, though your sewer agency can confirm the exact boundary. A camera run before closing shows the real condition of that pipe, which a home inspection usually cannot see.

Camera findings are described with the pipe condition terms that the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and the industry use. Ask for a copy of the video or the findings so you can use them when negotiating repairs. We give the summary in plain terms and the price for any repair we recommend.

The inspection itself is a planned visit, but if you see sewage backing up now, call the 24/7 line and ask for emergency help first. Most scheduled inspections can be booked for the same day when a crew is available.

Camera inspection questions

How long does a sewer camera inspection take?

Most inspections of a single family lateral are done in under an hour, once the cleanout is open and the line is clear.

Can I keep the video?

Ask when you book. We can share findings and clips so you have a record for a seller, an insurer or a later repair.

Do you inspect before a home purchase?

Yes. Many buyers schedule a camera run during their inspection period. Tell us the closing timeline when you call.

What if the camera cannot pass a blockage?

Then the line needs cleaning first. We clear it, then complete the inspection in the same visit when time allows.
