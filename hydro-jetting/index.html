Sewer backup: what to do first

A line that backs up into floor drains or tubs needs attention right away, and our 24/7 line takes that call at any hour. While you wait, stop all water use and keep people and pets away from any standing wastewater.

Jetting is often booked as the follow up to a first clearing, and same-day scheduling is available when a crew is free.

  1. Turn off dishwashers and washing machines and avoid flushing.
  2. Keep children and pets out of any wet area, since backed up wastewater carries bacteria.
  3. Do not pour chemicals into the line. They do not clear grease or roots and they stay in the pipe.
  4. Open the yard cleanout cap only if you know where it is, and leave it to the plumber if water is standing in the pipe.

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A cable cuts a channel through a clog. The water around the cut often stays coated with grease, scale and sludge, which is why the same line can stop again within weeks.

Hydro jetting sends water through a nozzle with rear facing jets. The jets pull the nozzle forward and spray the entire pipe wall as it moves. The debris flushes downstream with the water, and the pipe is left close to its full width.

When jetting is the right call

  • Grease buildup in a kitchen line, common in homes that cook often and in restaurants.
  • Mineral scale and soap curd, which build faster in very hard water.
  • Fine root growth entering joints in older sewer laterals.
  • A main line that has been cabled several times in a short span.
  • Routine maintenance on a restaurant or multi unit building line.

Jetting is not for every pipe. A line with a collapsed section, a large offset or a broken joint can be damaged by pressure, so we look first.

Why we put a camera in first

Before a jet goes in, a camera run shows the pipe material, the joints and any damage. Clay, cast iron and thin walled older pipe each tolerate pressure differently. If the camera shows a crack, the better step is repair, and our sewer line repair page explains those options. Drain and sewer work follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as California adopts it.

The same inspection also gives you a record of the pipe's condition. A sewer camera inspection is a separate service when you want the findings without jetting.

Hard water and drain lines in Laguna Hills

The El Toro Water District's 2026 water quality report lists hardness of about 236 mg/L for treated imported water and about 293 mg/L for water from the Baker plant. The USGS calls anything above 180 mg/L very hard.

Moulton Niguel Water District, which also serves part of Laguna Hills, reported an average of about 16.65 grains per gallon in its 2022 report. Water this hard leaves mineral film in drains, and jetting removes film that cables leave behind.

What a jetting visit looks like

  1. Inspect

    A camera checks the pipe so the jetting pressure matches the material.

  2. Access

    We open the cleanout and set the hose and nozzle in the line.

  3. Jet

    The nozzle works forward and back along the pipe while water flushes debris out.

  4. Verify

    A second camera pass or a flow test confirms the pipe is clean.

Hydro Jetting questions, answered

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. A severe backup is cleared first with a cable if needed, and jetting follows as soon as a crew and equipment are available.

Will hydro jetting damage my pipes?

On sound pipe it does not, because the pressure is matched to the material. We run a camera first, and we do not jet pipe that is collapsed or badly cracked.

How often should a restaurant line be jetted?

It depends on volume and grease control. Many kitchens schedule jetting several times a year, and a camera check after the first visit shows how fast the line fills.

Is jetting better than snaking?

For a single clog a cable is faster and costs less. For grease, scale or repeat stoppages, jetting cleans more of the pipe wall.
