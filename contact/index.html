Prepare

What to tell the dispatcher

A few details help us plan the first step and send the right equipment.

Have these ready when you call

  • The street address and ZIP code, and any gate code or parking notes.
  • What is happening: leak, backup, no hot water, smell or noise.
  • Where the water is coming from and whether you have shut it off.
  • The type and age of the water heater if it is involved.
  • Whether you rent or own, and your Mutual or property manager if you live in Laguna Woods Village.

If you smell gas, leave the building first and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe place. Call 911 if there is danger.
