What Is a Plumb Line Used For in Laguna Hills, CA?

A plumb line is a weight on a string that hangs straight down. The word plumbing comes from the same root, and the tool still helps plumbers set pipes and fixtures straight.

By the Laguna Hills Express Plumbing team. Published October 23, 2026.

Call (949) 325-3122 now.

A plumb line is one of the oldest tools in construction. A pointed weight, called a bob, hangs from a string, and gravity pulls it into a perfectly vertical line. The word plumb comes from the Latin word for lead, the metal the bobs were made of, and the plumber trade takes its name from it.

Modern plumbers also use a level, a laser and a torpedo level. The plumb line is still handy because it needs no battery and it shows true vertical over a long drop.

What plumbers use it for

  • Checking that a vertical drain stack or vent runs straight.
  • Lining up a toilet flange with the wall and the fixture above.
  • Setting a tub or shower valve at the right depth.
  • Marking where a pipe should pass through several floors.
  • Confirming that a wall, post or cabinet is out of plumb before fitting a fixture.

Vertical stacks are checked against the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as California adopts it. A pipe that is not plumb is not always a problem. A drain is meant to slope, so horizontal lines are checked for fall, not for level.

Plumb, level and slope

Three terms that are easy to mix up
TermMeaningWhere it matters
PlumbExactly verticalStacks, vents, risers and wall mounted fixtures.
LevelExactly horizontalFixtures, shelves and tub rims.
SlopeA set drop over a set runHorizontal drain lines, commonly one quarter inch per foot.

A drain that is level will not carry waste well, and a drain that slopes too steeply can leave solids behind. The right slope carries both liquid and solids.

How to use a plumb line

  1. Hang

    Tie the string to a nail or a mark above your target and let the bob hang free.

  2. Settle

    Wait until the bob stops swinging, or dip it in a cup of water to steady it.

  3. Mark

    Mark the wall or floor directly under the point of the bob.

  4. Compare

    Check the pipe or fixture against the string, and adjust until they line up.

Wind makes a bob swing, so use it indoors or in shelter. A short string swings less than a long one.

Why plumb matters for fixtures

A toilet that sits out of plumb can rock, and its seal can fail. A tub set crooked leaves water pooling at one end. A wall mounted sink pulled off the vertical puts stress on the drain and supply connections.

That is why we check alignment before the finish work, when changes are easy. For a full remodel, plumbing installation explains how the planning works.

Plumb lines in the age of lasers

Laser levels project a straight vertical or horizontal line across a room, and they are faster for long runs. They need a battery and can wash out in bright sun. A plumb bob costs little, never fails and is accurate on a vertical drop of any height.

Many plumbers carry both. They use the laser to line up fixtures across a wall and the bob to check a single stack through a floor opening.

A short history of the plumb bob

Builders have used plumb bobs for thousands of years. Egyptian, Greek and Roman builders hung weights from cords to check walls and columns. The tool is simple and has barely changed because the physics behind it does not change.

The word plumber comes from the same Latin root, since lead pipes were once the trade's main product. Today the pipes are copper, plastic and steel, but the name stuck.

Laguna Hills notes: choose a licensed contractor and plan the hours

Plumbing contractors in California hold the C-36 classification from the Contractors State License Board. The board lets anyone check a license at cslb.ca.gov by license number or business name, which is worth doing before you hire.

The City of Laguna Hills sets construction hours of Monday through Friday from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. and Saturday from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Laguna Woods Village hours are shorter, at Monday through Friday from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., so plan noisy work around them.

Plumb checks you can do at home

Quick checks before a fixture install

  • Check that the wall behind a sink or toilet is flat and plumb.
  • Measure from the plumb mark to the flange to confirm the rough in.
  • Hang a bob inside a stack cleanout to see how straight it runs.
  • Test shelves and cabinets with a level before fitting them.

Local questions, answered

Is a plumb line the same as a level?

No. A plumb line shows vertical. A level shows horizontal. A level can also show vertical when held against a wall.

Do drain pipes need to be plumb?

Vertical stacks should be plumb. Horizontal drain lines need a slope, commonly one quarter inch per foot.

How do I check a contractor's license in Laguna Hills?

Use the Contractors State License Board's online lookup at cslb.ca.gov. Search by license number or business name and confirm the plumbing classification, C-36.

Are there limits on construction hours in Laguna Hills?

The city allows work on weekdays from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. and on Saturdays from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Laguna Woods Village has shorter hours.
