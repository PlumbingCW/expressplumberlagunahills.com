How to Get a Clogged Toilet to Flush: What Laguna Hills, CA Homes Need to Know

A toilet that will not flush is usually a local clog you can clear in minutes. This guide shows the safe order of steps and how to tell when the problem is further down the line.

By the Laguna Hills Express Plumbing team. Published November 4, 2026.

Call (949) 325-3122 now.

A clogged toilet is one of the most common household plumbing problems. Most clogs sit in the trap way of the bowl, a few inches past the water line. A plunger or a toilet auger reaches that spot.

A few steps done in the right order prevent an overflow and save the call.

Before you try to flush again

What to do first when the bowl is high

  • Do not flush again. Another flush adds water to a bowl that is already full.
  • Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop more water from entering.
  • Close the supply valve behind the toilet if the bowl is rising.
  • Put towels on the floor and open a window if the room is small.
  • Wear gloves and avoid splashes.

Clearing the clog in order

  1. Plunge

    Use a flange plunger, which has a rubber flap that fits the drain. Seat it and push down slowly, then pull up with force. Repeat for a minute.

  2. Soak

    Add a squirt of dish soap and warm water, not boiling, and wait ten minutes.

  3. Auger

    Feed a toilet auger into the drain and crank to break up or retrieve the clog.

  4. Test

    Flush once. If the bowl rises, close the valve and stop.

Never mix drain chemicals in a toilet. The liquids can splash, and they do not clear most toilet clogs. Toilet and drain work follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as California adopts it.

What usually causes the clog

  • Too much toilet paper at once.
  • Wipes labeled flushable that do not break down.
  • Toys, cotton swabs and feminine products.
  • Low flow toilets with a weak flush and a narrow trap way.
  • A partial blockage further down the drain line.

If the same toilet clogs repeatedly, the problem may be beyond the bowl. A weak flush from a worn flapper or clogged rim jets is also common, and toilet repair covers those fixes.

If you see these signs, our drain cleaning team clears the line. A main line stoppage is an emergency, and the 24/7 line is open.

If the water keeps rising

If the bowl keeps rising after you have stopped flushing, close the supply valve and lift the tank lid. Push the flapper down to stop water from entering. Then wait. A bowl often settles a little as the water seeps past the clog.

A plumber uses a closet auger that protects the porcelain, and a camera if the clog does not clear. A toilet that clogs again right after clearing may have an object in the trap or a problem in the line behind it.

Teach children early that only paper goes in the toilet. Small toys are a common cause of stubborn clogs, and they can lodge in the trap where a plunger cannot move them.

What a plumber does that you cannot

A plumber carries a closet auger with a protective sleeve, a drum machine for lines beyond the toilet and a camera for clogs that keep returning. The camera shows whether the trouble is a foreign object, a root intrusion or a broken pipe.

In some homes the toilet must be pulled off the floor to reach a blockage in the trap. We reset it with a new seal afterward, and we check the flange for damage while it is off.

Laguna Hills and Laguna Woods notes: permits and sewer service

The City of Laguna Hills handout of projects exempt from permits lists faucet or toilet repair or replacement, so clearing a clog or swapping a toilet is not a permit matter. Moving a toilet to a new spot is different, and the Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 can advise.

In Laguna Woods, the El Toro Water District provides all water and wastewater service. If a street sewer main is blocked, the district that serves your address is the one to call, and your plumber can tell you whether the problem is on your side of the property line.

Sources: City of Laguna Hills exempt projects handout, El Toro Water District service area information.

Preventing the next clog

Flush only toilet paper and human waste. Keep a toilet plunger and a toilet auger in the bathroom. Teach everyone in the house which valve closes the supply.

If you live in Laguna Niguel or Laguna Woods, the same steps apply, and our crew covers both cities.

Local questions, answered

What is the difference between a flange plunger and a cup plunger?

A flange plunger has an extra rubber flap that seals in the toilet drain. A cup plunger is flat and suits sinks and tubs.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaner in a toilet?

We advise against it. It rarely clears a toilet clog and can splash on skin and damage fixtures.

Who do I call if the sewer main is blocked in Laguna Woods?

The El Toro Water District provides water and wastewater service there. A plumber can first confirm whether the blockage is in your line.

Does clearing a toilet clog need a permit in Laguna Hills?

No. Clearing a clog is a service call, and the city's handout lists toilet repair or replacement as exempt.
