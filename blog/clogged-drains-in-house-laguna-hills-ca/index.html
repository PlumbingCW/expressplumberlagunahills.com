Clogged Drains in the House: A Laguna Hills, CA Homeowner Guide

Most clogged drains build slowly from hair, grease and mineral film. This guide covers what causes them, what you can safely try and when to call.

By the Laguna Hills Express Plumbing team. Published November 8, 2026.

Call (949) 325-3122 now.

A clogged drain rarely starts as a full blockage. It starts as a slow drain that you adjust to. A little hair here, a layer of grease there, and one day the water stops moving.

Where the clog sits decides what will clear it. A fixture clog responds to a plunger or a small auger. A line clog needs a machine.

What clogs household drains

Common clog sources by room
RoomUsual causeWhat helps
Bathroom sinkHair, toothpaste and soap curdClean the stopper, flush with hot water.
Shower and tubHair and soap filmA drain hair catcher, then a small auger.
Kitchen sinkGrease, food scraps and starchesHot water, a plunger, then a cable.
LaundryLint and detergent residueA lint filter and a periodic flush.
Main lineRoots, grease and wipesProfessional cable or jetting.

Grease is the worst offender in kitchens. It leaves the sink as liquid, cools in the pipe and builds up, trapping food and soap.

What you can safely try

  1. Clear the stopper

    Remove and clean the stopper or the strainer, which often holds the clog.

  2. Plunge

    Cover the overflow, seat the plunger and pump firmly.

  3. Auger

    Feed a hand auger, turn it to catch the clog and pull it back.

  4. Flush

    Run hot water for a minute to carry loose debris away.

Skip store bought chemical cleaners. They can damage pipes and harm skin, and they do little against grease or hair that has set. Drain work in California follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as the state adopts it.

When it is more than a fixture clog

  • More than one fixture drains slowly at once.
  • A toilet gurgles when the washer runs.
  • Water backs up into a tub or floor drain.
  • The same drain clogs again within weeks.
  • A sewage smell near the lowest drain in the house.

These point to a line problem. Our drain cleaning service uses cables and cameras, and hydro jetting cleans a pipe wall of grease and scale.

Room by room tips

In the kitchen, scrape plates into the trash and wipe pans before washing. A sink strainer catches scraps that would otherwise settle in the trap. In the bathroom, a hair catcher in the tub and a quick pull of the sink stopper every month prevent most slow drains.

In the laundry, a mesh filter on the washing machine hose catches lint. Heavy loads of towels shed more, so clean the filter more often after them.

What a professional does differently

A plumber matches the tool to the clog. A hand auger clears a trap, a drum machine clears a branch line, and a jetter cleans a pipe wall. A camera shows what is inside when the cause is unclear.

That is why a clog that returns after a cable clears it deserves a look with a camera. The cable may have opened a hole in a coating that is still on the walls of the pipe.

How often drains should be cleaned

A drain that runs freely does not need cleaning. A drain that has needed a plunger twice in a year is telling you something about the pipe behind it. Kitchen lines in busy homes benefit from a scheduled cleaning every year or two.

Pouring boiling water down a plastic drain pipe can soften joints, so use hot tap water instead. Enzyme cleaners break down organic film slowly and are safe for pipes, but they do not clear a solid clog.

A clog that is not in your house

Sometimes the blockage is beyond your property line. If every drain in the house backs up and neighbors have the same trouble, the sewer main in the street may be blocked. In that case the sewer agency that serves your address handles the repair, and a plumber can confirm which side of the line the problem sits on.

A quick check helps. Look at the cleanout near the house or at the property line. If water stands high in the cleanout, the blockage is downstream of it, and that is a reason to call right away.

Laguna Hills notes: older drains and very hard water

Laguna Hills grew from Moulton Ranch land that was subdivided in the early 1960s. Homes built in the decades after often have older drain lines, which gather more buildup than newer plastic pipe and can allow roots into joints.

The El Toro Water District's 2026 report lists hardness of about 236 to 293 mg/L, and Moulton Niguel Water District reported an average of about 16.65 grains per gallon in 2022. The USGS calls water this hard very hard, and it leaves mineral film that catches hair and soap.

Sources: City of Laguna Hills history, El Toro Water District 2026 Water Quality Report, Moulton Niguel Water District 2022 Water Quality Report, USGS.

Habits that keep drains open

Simple habits for clear drains

  • Wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing.
  • Use hair catchers in tubs and showers.
  • Run hot water for a minute after washing dishes.
  • Keep wipes, coffee grounds and cooking oil out of drains.
  • Flush a rarely used drain with water every few weeks.

Local questions, answered

Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging?

Grease and food starch build up on pipe walls. A cable clears the clog, but jetting removes the coating that causes it to return.

Does hard water make clogs worse?

It can. Mineral film narrows the pipe and holds hair and soap, which is relevant here, since local water is very hard.

Is it safe to use baking soda and vinegar?

It is gentle and safe, but it rarely clears a real clog. It can freshen a drain between services.

When is a clogged drain an emergency?

When water backs up into a tub or floor drain, or when several fixtures stop at once. Call the 24/7 line.
