Plumbing Installation in Laguna Hills, CA

Remodels, additions and new fixtures all need pipe that is sized, vented and supported correctly. Call to schedule an installation, and we walk the job, explain the plan and quote the price before work begins.

Installation covers work that adds or changes plumbing, such as a new bathroom, a relocated sink, a laundry hookup or a replaced fixture set. It differs from repair because the layout changes and the work is usually planned in advance.

Good installation starts with a drawing of the routes, sizes and vents, and ends with a pressure test and inspection.

Common installation jobs

  • Bathroom and kitchen remodels with new supply and drain lines.
  • Sink, tub, shower and toilet installations.
  • Washing machine, dishwasher and ice maker hookups.
  • Hose bibs, outdoor sinks and irrigation tie ins.
  • Water heater, softener and filter connections.
  • Gas appliance connections and stub outs.

Specific jobs have their own pages. See toilet repair and installation, faucet repair, garbage disposal installation and water heater installation.

How drain, waste and vent systems work

Every fixture needs a drain sloped to carry waste away, a trap that holds a water seal, and a vent that lets air in behind the flow. Without the vent the trap can be siphoned dry, and sewer gas enters the home.

Vent sizing and routing are common reasons plans fail inspection. We lay out the vents before pipe is cut, so the walls are opened once.

Gas lines: a safety first note

If you smell gas, leave the building before you call anyone. Do not use switches, phones inside the home or open flames, and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe place. Call 911 if there is danger.

Gas line work needs a pressure test and inspection before it is concealed. We plan gas connections as part of a larger job and confirm what your city requires.

Homes on a septic tank are rare in this part of Orange County, but a few older properties still have one. Septic work is regulated separately, so we confirm what applies before any quote.

Permits and inspections

New fixtures, new lines and moved drains generally need a permit. The Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 can confirm what applies, and it applies the 2025 California Building Standards Code to applications filed on or after January 1, 2026.

Work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Like for like faucet or toilet swaps are listed as exempt in the city's handout.

How an installation project runs

  1. Walk

    We look at the space, the existing lines and the fixtures you chose.

  2. Plan

    You get a route, a scope and a price before any work starts.

  3. Build

    Rough plumbing goes in, is pressure tested and is inspected.

  4. Finish

    Fixtures are set, tested and left clean, and we review shutoffs with you.

If the plumbing in the house is old, a repiping plan may fit better than adding to worn lines.

Plumbing Installation questions, answered

Do I need a permit to add a fixture?

Usually yes. Ask the Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600, or tell us your address and we confirm it for you.

Can you work with my remodel contractor?

Yes. We coordinate rough in and finish timing so walls are open only as long as needed.

Do you supply fixtures?

We can supply them or install ones you buy. Send us the model numbers so we check rough in measurements.

How do I schedule?

Call the line and describe the job. We book a walkthrough, often the same day when a crew is available.
