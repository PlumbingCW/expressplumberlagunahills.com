Sewer line failure: what to do first

A sewer line that is failing can send wastewater into your tub or floor drain, and our 24/7 line is open for that call. While you wait, stop using water and keep everyone away from standing wastewater.

Do not try to dig or break pipe yourself. A camera visit tells us whether the problem is a clog or a damaged line, and same-day service is offered when a crew is available.

  1. Stop water use in the whole house, including laundry and dishwashers.
  2. Keep children and pets out of wet areas and wash hands after any contact.
  3. Do not pour chemicals into the line, since they do not fix a broken pipe.
  4. If wastewater is entering a room, switch off power to nearby outlets only if the breaker is dry and safe to reach.

How sewer lines fail in older Orange County neighborhoods

The Moulton Ranch land that became Laguna Hills was subdivided in the early 1960s. Homes from that period often have clay, cast iron or early plastic sewer laterals, and each material ages in its own way.

Clay joints open and let in roots. Cast iron scales inside and can crack near fittings. Soil movement can leave a low section where waste settles, which plumbers call a belly.

Signs your sewer lateral needs repair

  • Repeated main line clogs that return after cabling.
  • Gurgling toilets or slow drains in several fixtures at once.
  • A sewage smell near the cleanout or in the yard.
  • Wet or unusually green patches over the sewer path.
  • Cracks in a slab or settling near a drain stub.

These signs can also point to a heavy clog, so a camera inspection comes before any repair is proposed.

Repair options and honest trade-offs

Common sewer repair methods
MethodHow it worksTrade-off
Spot repairDig and replace one damaged sectionFastest for one defect, leaves the rest of the pipe as it was.
Open trench replacementExcavate and install new pipe along the old pathMost certain result, but it disturbs landscaping and hardscape.
Pipe liningA resin lined sleeve is cured inside the old pipeLess digging, but needs a pipe that is not collapsed and loses a little diameter.
Pipe burstingA new pipe is pulled in as the old one is splitLittle surface disruption, needs entry and exit pits.

Which method fits depends on the camera findings, the pipe depth and what sits above it. We explain the options in plain terms and give the price for the one we recommend.

Cast iron sewer pipe: what cracking looks like

Cast iron was common in drain and sewer lines for decades. It scales inside, which narrows the pipe, and it can crack along its length or split at a hub joint. A camera shows rust nodules, flaking walls and hairline cracks.

A short cracked run can be cut out and replaced, or sleeved with a repair sleeve that clamps over the damage. A pipe that is flaking along its whole length is usually better replaced.

Permits, inspections and city rules

Replacing sewer pipe is a permitted type of work in most California cities. Ask the Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 which permit and inspection steps apply to your address.

Laguna Hills applies the 2025 California Building Standards Code to applications filed on or after January 1, 2026. Sewer work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Sewer Line Repair questions, answered

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. We clear the immediate backup first, then camera the line to see whether a repair is needed.

How do I know whether I need repair or just cleaning?

A camera inspection answers that. If the pipe is intact and only coated or blocked, cleaning is enough. Cracks, roots in joints or a sagging section call for repair.

Can a sewer line be fixed without digging up my yard?

Sometimes. Lining and bursting reduce digging when the pipe is a good candidate. The camera findings decide which method is safe for your line.

Does repair need a permit?

Replacing sewer pipe often does. Check with the Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 for your address.
