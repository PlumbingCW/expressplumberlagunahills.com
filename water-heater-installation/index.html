A new installation covers more than setting a tank in place. The heater needs the right size, a safe location, correct water and gas or electrical connections, and the extras the code requires.

This page covers planned work. If your heater has already failed, water heater replacement is the faster path.

Sizing and fuel choice

Household size, the number of bathrooms and peak demand decide the right capacity. A tank that matches your peak morning use will run out less often than one chosen on price alone.

Fuel and type choices for a new heater
OptionGood fitWhat to check
Gas tankHome already has gas and a ventVent size and slope, combustion air.
Electric tankNo gas line, or a closet installCircuit size and breaker capacity.

Code details we build into every install

Laguna Hills publishes a water heater handout based on the 2022 California Plumbing Code. It calls for seismic straps in the top and bottom thirds of the tank, with the bottom strap at least 4 inches above the controls.

  • An expansion tank on closed systems.
  • A temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge line piped to a safe place.
  • A drain pan with a drain where leakage could damage the structure.
  • Venting where the horizontal run does not exceed 75 percent of the vertical height.
  • Enough combustion air in a closet or garage.

Laguna Niguel's handout adds that gas vents slope upward at one quarter inch per foot and that strapping may use a State Architect accepted kit. We follow the rules of the city where the home sits.

Permits for new installations

Ask the Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 whether your installation needs a permit and an inspection. The division applies the 2025 California Building Standards Code to applications filed on or after January 1, 2026.

In Laguna Woods the city requires a permit for all water heater replacements, and Laguna Woods Village adds its own approvals. We handle the paperwork when we take the job.

Where the heater goes

Garages, closets, attics and exterior enclosures each have rules for clearance and access. In a garage, a heater with a pilot or burner is generally raised so the ignition source sits at least 18 inches above the floor. An attic unit needs a pan and a safe drain.

We check access for service and for future removal, because a heater that is hard to reach costs more to maintain.

Installation day

  1. Prepare

    We confirm the plan, set out materials and shut off water and fuel.

  2. Install

    The heater is set, strapped, vented and connected to water and fuel or power.

  3. Protect

    Relief valve, expansion tank and drain pan go in as required.

  4. Commission

    We test the system, set the temperature and walk through the shutoffs with you.

Water hardness here is very high by USGS standards, so ask us about a water softener to protect the new heater from scale.

Water Heater Installation questions, answered

How long does a water heater installation take?

A standard tank installation often takes a few hours. A new vent, a fuel change or an inspection can lengthen the job.

Do you remove my old water heater?

Yes. Removal and disposal are part of the job, and we explain the price before we start.

Do I need a permit for a new water heater?

Often yes. Ask the Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 about your address. We handle the permit paperwork.

Can you install a heater in the garage?

Yes. The heater must be strapped and protected from impact, and the burner height or a platform is checked against code.
