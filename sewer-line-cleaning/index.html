Main line blockage: what to do while you wait

When the main sewer line blocks, the lowest drain in the house fills first, and our 24/7 line is open for that call. While you wait, stop all water use and leave the toilets alone.

Cleaning a main line is often possible the same day, depending on crew availability and access to the cleanout.

  1. Do not run the washing machine, dishwasher or shower.
  2. Keep the area around the lowest drain clear of rugs and stored items.
  3. Find the cleanout, usually a capped pipe near the house or at the property line, and keep it accessible.
  4. Tell the dispatcher if wastewater is already on a floor.

Main line cleaning is different from clearing a sink

A main sewer line is larger, longer and deeper than a branch drain. It needs a heavy cable machine or a jetting unit, and it is cleaned from the yard or house cleanout rather than from a fixture.

The cutting head, the cable size and the amount of line fed in all depend on the pipe diameter and the blockage. A small drain machine cannot reach or clear a main.

What blocks a main sewer line

  • Tree and shrub roots entering joints in older clay or cast iron laterals.
  • Grease from kitchen drains that cools and hardens downstream.
  • Wipes and other non flushable items that catch on rough joints.
  • Scale and sludge that build up on pipe walls over many years.
  • A bellied section where waste settles and collects.

How we clean the main line

We start at the cleanout and feed a heavy cable with a cutting head until it clears the blockage. Where roots are the cause, we use a root cutter and then follow with a second pass to clean the pipe wall.

For grease and scale, hydro jetting gives a cleaner result than cable alone. When the same stoppage returns, we recommend a camera inspection to find the cause.

A schedule that keeps roots and grease in check

Lines that collect roots usually need cleaning on a regular cycle, and the right interval depends on what the camera shows. A line with light root intrusion may be cleaned every year or two. A line with heavy growth may need a repair instead.

Sewer Line Cleaning questions, answered

Do you clean main sewer lines at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. A blocked main gets priority, and we book same-day service when a crew is available.

What is the difference between sewer line cleaning and drain cleaning?

Drain cleaning clears fixtures and branch lines. Sewer line cleaning clears the main line that carries all the home's waste to the street, using heavier equipment.

How do I find my sewer cleanout?

Look for a capped pipe, usually 3 or 4 inches across, near the house or close to the property line. If you cannot find it, tell the dispatcher and we will look when we arrive.

Will the blockage come back?

It can if roots, a belly or a crack caused it. A camera pass after cleaning shows the cause so you can decide on a repair.
