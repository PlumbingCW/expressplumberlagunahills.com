What to do when a pipe bursts and water floods

A burst pipe is always an emergency, and our line is open 24/7. While you wait for the visit, shut off the main water valve and open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line.

Same-day and after-hours service is offered when a crew is free, and the first minutes matter most for limiting damage.

  1. Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. Check near the water meter, in the garage or along the front wall.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain remaining water.
  3. Stay away from standing water near outlets and appliances. Switch off the breaker only if you can reach it dry.
  4. Take photos of the damage, then move belongings out of the wet area.

Where to find your main shutoff

Everyone in the house should know where the main valve is. It is often a round wheel handle or a lever on the supply pipe where it enters the house. Some homes also have a valve at the meter box in the front yard.

If the valve is stuck or leaks when turned, tell the dispatcher. We can bring a replacement, and we may shut off at the meter if your district allows it.

Why pipes burst in Southern California homes

Hard freezes are rare in Laguna Hills, so most bursts come from other causes. Corrosion thins the pipe wall until it gives way. Water pressure that runs high strains joints and fittings.

  • Old galvanized or copper pipe that has corroded from the inside.
  • Water pressure above the recommended range for home plumbing.
  • Joints and fittings that were poorly soldered or glued.
  • Ground movement or construction work that stresses a line.
  • Water hammer from valves that close too fast.

What a repair looks like

  1. Stop the water

    We confirm the main is off and isolate the failed section.

  2. Open and assess

    We expose the damaged pipe and check the surrounding runs for weak spots.

  3. Repair

    We cut out the failed section and install new pipe and fittings that suit the existing material.

  4. Test

    We restore pressure, inspect every joint and confirm the system holds.

If the pipe has failed in more than one spot, repiping a run or the whole house can be a better value than repeated patches.

Cleanup and the next steps

Water that sits in walls and flooring can lead to mold within a couple of days. Drying the area quickly matters, and a water mitigation company handles that work. We stop the water and fix the pipe, and we can tell you what we saw behind the wall.

For homes where the burst came from a hidden line, a leak detection visit can check whether other lines are losing pressure.

Code and permits

Replacing a section of supply pipe follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). The Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 can confirm whether a permit applies to a larger run.

We give a clear price before work starts, and the final price depends on access, the pipe material and the hour of the call.

Burst Pipe Repair questions, answered

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. Shut off the main valve and call, and we book a visit as soon as a crew is available.

What should I do first when a pipe bursts?

Close the main water valve, open the lowest faucet to drain the line, and stay away from standing water near electrical items. Then call us.

Can you repair a burst pipe inside a wall?

Yes. We find the failed section, open the wall only as much as needed, and replace the pipe. You will need drywall repair afterward.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover it?

That depends on your policy. Take photos and keep receipts, and ask us for a written description of the cause and repair.
