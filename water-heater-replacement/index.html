Failed tank: what to do first

A tank that leaks from the shell has reached the end of its life, and our 24/7 line is open for a replacement call. While you wait, close the cold water valve above the heater and turn off its gas or power.

Replacement visits are offered the same day when a crew and a matching unit are available.

  1. Close the cold water shutoff at the top of the heater.
  2. Turn the gas control to off, or switch off the breaker for an electric unit.
  3. Open a hot tap in the house to relieve pressure and to help the tank drain if it is safe.
  4. Clear the area around the heater so the crew can reach it, and note the size printed on the label.

How to tell when replacement is the right call

  • Water leaking from the shell of the tank, not from a fitting.
  • Rusty hot water that does not clear after a flush.
  • A tank over roughly a decade old with repeated repairs.
  • Frequent popping or rumbling from heavy sediment.
  • Hot water that runs out far faster than it used to.

If the problem is a part, we say so and repair instead. See water heater repair for what is fixable.

What a code compliant replacement includes

Laguna Hills publishes its water heater requirements, which follow the 2022 California Plumbing Code. They cover seismic straps in the top and bottom thirds of the tank, with the lower strap at least 4 inches above the controls. They also cover an expansion tank on closed systems, a temperature and pressure relief valve piped to a safe location and a drain pan where needed.

Gas vents need the right slope and clearance, and the horizontal run of a vent may not exceed 75 percent of its vertical height. Combustion air must be adequate in a closet or garage.

Permits and the neighboring cities

Ask the Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 whether your replacement needs a permit. In Laguna Woods, the city requires a permit for every water heater replacement, and Village communities add their own approvals.

If your home sits in Laguna Woods or Laguna Niguel, tell us when you call so the paperwork matches that city.

What happens during the visit

  1. Confirm

    We verify the old unit, the fuel type, the vent and the shutoffs.

  2. Remove

    The old tank is drained, disconnected and hauled away.

  3. Set

    The new unit is placed with straps, valves, a relief line and, where required, an expansion tank.

  4. Test

    We fill, light or power the unit, check every connection and review temperature settings with you.

We give a clear price before the work starts, and it depends on the unit, the venting and the hour of the call.

Water Heater Replacement questions, answered

Do you replace water heaters at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. If a matching unit and a crew are available, we replace the heater the same day.

How long does a replacement take?

Many tank replacements take two to four hours. A fuel change, a new vent or a permit inspection can add time.

Do I need an expansion tank?

Closed systems generally need one under the California Plumbing Code. We check whether your system is closed and explain what applies.

Will you haul away the old heater?

Yes. Removal is part of the job, and we explain any extra charges before starting.

Can I switch from a tank to a tankless heater?

Usually. Gas size, venting and electrical needs are checked first. See the tankless page for what changes.
