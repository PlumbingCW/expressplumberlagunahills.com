When a clogged drain is an emergency

A drain that backs up into a tub, a shower or a floor drain is an emergency, and our line is open 24/7 for that call. While you wait for the visit, stop running water in the house and leave the toilets unflushed.

Same-day and after-hours visits are available when a crew is free, so a backup at night or on a weekend does not have to sit until Monday.

  1. Stop using sinks, tubs, dishwashers and the washing machine until the line is clear.
  2. Skip chemical drain cleaner on a drain that is already full. It can splash back and it sits in the pipe where the plumber has to work.
  3. Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the lowest drain in the house.
  4. Note which fixtures are slow or bubbling and tell us when you call.

Why drains clog in Laguna Hills homes

Many Laguna Hills homes were built in the decades after the Moulton Ranch land was subdivided in the early 1960s. Older drain lines are common here, and they carry years of grease, soap and hair on their walls. Roots from mature landscaping can also work into aging pipe joints.

Local water adds to the problem. The El Toro Water District reported treated water hardness of about 236 to 293 mg/L in its 2026 water quality report, which the USGS scale calls very hard. Hard water leaves mineral scale and soap curd that narrow a drain and catch hair.

How we clear a clogged drain

We start by finding the closest access point, which is usually a cleanout, a removable trap or a roof vent. A drum machine feeds a steel cable with a cutting head through the line until it breaks through the blockage.

  1. Locate

    We confirm which fixtures are affected and find the nearest cleanout or access point.

  2. Clear

    A cable with the right cutting head works through the blockage and pulls debris back out.

  3. Test

    We run water at every affected fixture to confirm the line flows freely.

  4. Advise

    If grease, roots or a damaged pipe caused the clog, we tell you what stops it from returning.

Cable, jetting or camera: picking the right tool

A cable is the fastest answer for a single clog. When a line clogs again within weeks, grease or scale is usually coating the pipe, and hydro jetting washes the full wall clean.

A camera shows what a cable cannot. If the same line keeps stopping, a sewer camera inspection can reveal a belly, a crack or roots before they cause a bigger backup. Heavy, repeated main line stoppages may call for sewer line cleaning instead.

Our home ZIP code, 92653, is where we answer the most emergency drain calls, and we also dispatch drain cleaning to 92654 and the neighboring cities listed on our service area page.

A kitchen sink that drains slowly is usually grease and food waste in the trap or the branch line. A bathroom sink clogs on hair and soap. If every fixture gurgles at once, the problem is in the main line and a single-fixture fix will not hold.

Permits and code on drain work

Clearing a clog with a cable does not change the plumbing, so it is a service call. Replacing or rerouting drain pipe is a different job, and the city's Building & Safety Division, reachable at (949) 707-2600, can say whether a permit applies.

The division applies the 2025 California Building Standards Code to applications filed on or after January 1, 2026. Drain work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Drain Cleaning questions, answered

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, and a backed up main line or overflowing fixture gets priority. Same-day visits are offered when a crew is available.

Is chemical drain cleaner safe to use first?

We advise against it on a drain that is already full. The liquid can splash during cabling and it can irritate skin, so tell the technician if you already used some.

How can I tell if the clog is in the main line?

If more than one fixture is slow, or a toilet gurgles when another fixture drains, the main line is a likely cause. Water rising in a floor drain or tub points the same way.
