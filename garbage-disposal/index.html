What to do when a disposal jams or leaks

A disposal that leaks into the cabinet, backs the sink up or trips the breaker is an emergency, and our 24/7 line is open for that call. While you wait, switch the disposal off at the wall and stop using the sink.

Same-day and after-hours visits are available when a crew is free.

  1. Switch off the disposal and unplug it or turn off its breaker before checking anything.
  2. Never put your hand into the disposal. Use tongs or pliers to remove an object you can see.
  3. Put a bowl under any drip and empty the cabinet to protect the floor of the cabinet.
  4. Do not run the disposal again until the jam is cleared.

Why disposals fail

Common causes are fibrous or starchy food, grease that cools and coats the walls, and hard items such as bones or utensils. Hard water scale can also build up and slow the shredder ring.

When the sink backs up past the disposal, the clog sits in the drain line, and our drain cleaning team clears it.

Installing a new garbage disposal

A new disposal mounts to the sink flange, connects to the drain and, in most kitchens, to the dishwasher drain. It also needs power from a switched outlet or a hardwired circuit.

  • Match the horsepower to how much you use the unit.
  • Check that the dishwasher drain hose loops high or uses an air gap.
  • Confirm the outlet or circuit is protected and sized for the motor.
  • Reconnect the trap with a proper slope toward the wall.

Disposal wiring follows the National Electrical Code (NEC), and drain connections follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Permits

A like for like swap of a disposal is routine, but new wiring or a moved drain is different. Ask the Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 whether your job needs a permit.

We give a clear price before work starts, and it depends on the unit you choose and whether wiring changes are needed.

Care that extends the life of a disposal

Run cold water while the unit grinds and for a few seconds after. Cut large scraps into small pieces and keep grease, pasta and coffee grounds out. If the unit smells, a handful of ice and a citrus peel cleans the chamber without chemicals.

Garbage Disposal questions, answered

Do you repair disposals at night or on weekends in Laguna Hills?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. Switch the disposal off, stop using the sink and call, and we book a visit when a crew is available.

Why does my disposal hum but not spin?

A jam is holding the grinder. Turn the unit off, free the flywheel with a hex key from below, then press the reset button.

Can a leaking disposal be fixed?

A leak at the flange or drain connection often can. A leak from the housing means the unit has failed and needs replacement.

Can you install a disposal I bought?

Yes. Tell us the model when you call so we bring the right mounting and drain parts.
