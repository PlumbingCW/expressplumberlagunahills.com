Tankless Water Heater Service in Laguna Hills, CA

A tankless heater gives continuous hot water, but very hard water and tight maintenance schedules decide how long it lasts. Call to schedule an install, a repair or a flush, and we explain the price before the work starts.

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows through a heat exchanger instead of storing a tank of hot water. It takes less space and can deliver hot water for as long as the flow lasts.

It also depends on clean water passages, correct venting and enough gas or power. When any of those slip, the symptoms are lukewarm water, error codes or short bursts of cold water.

Why tankless units need flushing in this area

Mineral scale collects in the heat exchanger and narrows the passages. The El Toro Water District reported hardness of about 236 mg/L for treated imported water and 293 mg/L for Baker plant water in 2026. The USGS scale calls that very hard.

Moulton Niguel Water District reported an average of about 16.65 grains per gallon in 2022. Water that hard builds scale quickly inside a tankless unit, which is why annual flushing matters.

What a flush involves

  1. Isolate

    We close the unit's water and gas valves and open the service ports on the isolation valves.

  2. Circulate

    A pump pushes a descaling solution through the heat exchanger for the time the maker recommends.

  3. Rinse

    We rinse the unit with clean water and check the inlet screen for debris.

  4. Verify

    We restore the valves, run hot water and confirm the temperature and flow.

A flush is a planned service. If a unit is already throwing codes, water heater repair covers diagnosis and part replacement.

Installing a tankless heater

A tankless install often needs a larger gas line and a specific vent type, along with electrical power for the controller. Ask us to check the existing gas pipe size against the unit's input rating before you buy.

What changes when you move from a tank to tankless
ItemTank heaterTankless heater
Gas line sizeUsually smallOften needs a larger line.
VentingStandard flueDedicated vent per the manufacturer.
MaintenanceAnnual drain and anode checkAnnual descaling flush.
Hot water supplyLimited by tank sizeContinuous at the unit's flow rate.

Code and permits

Replacing a tank with a tankless unit changes gas, vent and electrical work, so it is normally permitted. The Laguna Hills Building & Safety Division at (949) 707-2600 can confirm the steps for your address.

Gas work follows the California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). If you smell gas at any time, leave the home and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe place.

Choosing between service and replacement

A clean flush, a new inlet screen and a software reset fix many tankless complaints. A heat exchanger that is corroded or cracked is a replacement item. We give you both options when the choice is close.

For a planned swap from an older tank, see water heater installation.

Tankless Water Heater questions, answered

How often should a tankless water heater be flushed?

Many makers suggest once a year. In very hard water, a flush every six to twelve months is common, and your manual has the maker's guidance.

Why does my tankless heater show an error code?

Common causes are scale, a blocked vent, low gas pressure or a failed sensor. Tell us the code and the model when you call.

Is tankless worth it in Laguna Hills?

It depends on your household, gas supply and water. Continuous hot water is the main gain, and the hard water means you must commit to flushing.

Can a softener help my tankless unit?

Yes. Softened water reduces scale in the heat exchanger. See our water softener page for the local hardness figures.
