Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026.

This policy explains what information Laguna Hills Express Plumbing collects through this website and through calls to our line, and how we use it.

Information we collect

This website has no forms and no accounts. We do not ask you to enter personal information on the site. When you call (949) 325-3122, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of the plumbing problem.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information

We use call details to book and perform service, to answer questions about your visit and to improve how we handle calls. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and embedded content

This website does not set its own tracking cookies. Embedded Google Maps may set cookies under Google's own policies when they load. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing

We share information only with the people who need it to provide service, such as technicians and dispatchers, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing contact@expressplumberlagunahills.com. California residents may have additional rights under state law.
